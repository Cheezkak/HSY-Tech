Our Mission

Our goal is not just to “do the job” — we use technology to support worship and teaching, and enhance the overall experience of everyone attending youth. This team isn’t about filling a technical gap; it’s about serving our church and community with excellence.

Our Core Values

Faithful Stewardship

God has given each of us gifts, talents, and opportunities to serve. We want to use them faithfully and responsibly.

Teamwork

Our techs work together with one another, the worship band, and the speaker to ensure smooth and effective ministry.

Servant Leadership

We serve not to draw attention to ourselves, but to help others experience worship more fully and clearly.

Learning

Everyone starts somewhere. We encourage questions, practice, and growth through responsible experimentation.

Join The Team

Want to join our team as a volunteer? We’d love to have you! No tech experience is required, and we’ll help guide your learning. If this sounds like a fit, reach out to Liam or another team member.
